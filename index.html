Protocol Dura VR: Local Control Interface

This page is a locally-run replacement for the original Dura VR companion app, which no longer runs on modern Android. It talks directly to the drone's WiFi video/control protocol, reverse-engineered from decompiling the original APK and from packet captures of the app's real traffic. Everything here runs on this machine; the drone's WiFi access point has no internet access and no external server is involved. See DISCLAIMER.md for full safety, liability, and accuracy disclaimers.

Live video feed

The drone streams motion-JPEG frames over UDP to port 40000. This page's backend reassembles those frames and re-serves them below as an ordinary MJPEG-over-HTTP stream.

Protocol reference

All traffic (video, keepalive, telemetry, and control) is UDP between this host and the drone at 192.168.0.1:40000. Every packet starts with the two-byte magic 63 63, followed by a one-byte packet type.

Type Direction Meaning
0x01 client → drone heartbeat, sent every ~1s to keep the stream alive
0x01 drone → client heartbeat reply / device identification
0x03 drone → client video frame chunk (MJPEG, chunked to ~1454 bytes)
0x0a client → drone joystick / button control, 18 bytes
0x0b drone → client telemetry, 15 bytes, streamed continuously (~10Hz)

Control packet (type 0x0a) byte layout:

Offset Size Field
0–1 2 magic 63 63
2 1 type 0x0a
3–4 2 reserved, 00 00
5–6 2 constant 0b 00
7 1 constant 0x66
8 1 right stick, left/right (confirmed: increasing value = right)
9 1 right stick, up/down (confirmed: increasing value = up)
10 1 left stick, up/down (confirmed: increasing value = up)
11 1 left stick, left/right (confirmed: increasing value = right)
12 1 forward/backward (pitch) trimmer, between the two sticks. Sticky (holds its value rather than springing back to 0x80), moves in steps of 2.
13 1 bank (roll) trimmer, below the right stick. Sticky, steps of 2.
14 1 turn (yaw) trimmer, below the left stick. Sticky, steps of 2.
15 1 a baseline value, plus optional action bits OR'd on top. Baseline is 0x0c normally, or 0x04 while high-speed mode is active (these two are alternatives, never combined). Action bits, OR'd onto whichever baseline is current: +0x10 takeoff, +0x20 landing, +0x40 emergency stop (motor cutoff, drops the drone immediately), +0x02 compass mode active. Takeoff/ stop/land pulse briefly; the high-speed baseline and compass mode are sticky, they persist until toggled off again.
16 1 checksum: XOR of bytes 8–15
17 1 constant 0x99

All 4 axis bytes are centered at 0x80 (neutral), range 0x00–0xFF. Which byte is which physical stick axis is verified. Whether moving a stick in that direction makes the drone move the way you'd expect follows standard convention has not been independently verified by an actual flight.

Telemetry packet (type 0x0b) byte layout:

Offset Size Field
0–1 2 magic 63 63
2 1 type 0x0b
3–4 2 reserved, 00 00
5–6 2 constant 0f 00 (packet length, 15)
7 1 constant 0x66
8 1 battery, verified: decreases slowly and monotonically, never observed to increase or reset.
9 1 altitude, not yet verified. Tracked the original app's displayed altitude closely in one comparison (displayed -0.9, raw reading -1), though not matched byte-exactly.
10 1 gyro/vibration, not yet verified.
11–12 2 constant 00 00
13 1 checksum: XOR of bytes 8–12
14 1 constant 0x99
