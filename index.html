Protocol Dura VR: Local Control Interface

This page is a locally-run replacement for the original Dura VR companion app, which no longer runs on modern Android. It talks directly to the drone's WiFi video/control protocol, reverse-engineered from decompiling the original APK and from packet captures of the app's real traffic. Everything here runs on this machine; the drone's WiFi access point has no internet access and no external server is involved.

Live video feed

The drone streams motion-JPEG frames over UDP to port 40000. This page's backend reassembles those frames and re-serves them below as an ordinary MJPEG-over-HTTP stream.

Protocol reference

All traffic (video, keepalive, telemetry, and control) is UDP between this host and the drone at 192.168.0.1:40000. Every packet starts with the two-byte magic 63 63, followed by a one-byte packet type.

Type Direction Meaning
0x01 client → drone heartbeat, sent every ~1s to keep the stream alive
0x01 drone → client heartbeat reply / device identification
0x03 drone → client video frame chunk (MJPEG, chunked to ~1454 bytes)
0x0a client → drone joystick / button control, 18 bytes
0x0b drone → client telemetry, 15 bytes, streamed continuously (~10Hz)

Control packet (type 0x0a) byte layout:

Offset Size Field
0–1 2 magic 63 63
2 1 type 0x0a
3–4 2 reserved, 00 00
5–6 2 constant 0b 00
7 1 constant 0x66
8 1 axis A (paired with axis B)
9 1 axis B
10 1 axis C (paired with axis D)
11 1 axis D
12–14 3 constant 80 80 80 (unused axis)
15 1 flags: base 0x0c; +0x10 takeoff; +0x40 land
16 1 checksum: XOR of bytes 8–15
17 1 constant 0x99

All 4 axis bytes are centered at 0x80 (neutral), range 0x00–0xFF. The mapping from axis byte to physical stick direction is a best guess pending live confirmation.

Telemetry packet (type 0x0b) byte layout:

Offset Size Field
0–1 2 magic 63 63
2 1 type 0x0b
3–4 2 reserved, 00 00
5–6 2 constant 0f 00 (packet length, 15)
7 1 constant 0x66
8 1 constant 0x64
9–10 2 live value. Byte 10 (signed) is the part that actually changes, observed drifting slowly in roughly the -60..0 range while grounded and idle. Meaning unconfirmed (candidate: a gyro/rate or vertical-speed reading). This is what's shown live above.
11–12 2 constant 00 00
13 1 checksum: XOR of bytes 8–12
14 1 constant 0x99
